When generating or editing performance reviews and self-assessments in this project:

## Format Standards
- Start every review with: Employee Name, Review Period, Role, Reviewer
- Use markdown headers for each competency area
- Include specific examples and metrics for every claim
- Format dates as YYYY-MM-DD

## Required Sections
1. Impact & Deliverables — concrete accomplishments with measurable outcomes
2. Collaboration & Teamwork — cross-team contributions and partnership examples
3. Growth & Development — skills learned, certifications, stretch assignments
4. Challenges & Lessons Learned — honest reflection, not just positives
5. Goals for Next Quarter — 3-5 SMART goals

## Tone & Language
- Use first person for self-assessments, third person for peer/manager reviews
- Be specific: "Led the migration of 3 services to Kubernetes, reducing deployment time by 40%" not "Helped with infrastructure"
- Balance strengths with growth areas — every review should include at least one development opportunity
- Avoid superlatives ("best ever", "always") — use evidence-based language
- Keep each section to 3-5 bullet points maximum
- When referencing projects, include a link or ticket number where possible
